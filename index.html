Know how much shelf life each batch really has left.

ShelfTwin follows every batch through the cold chain, tells each store and distributor each morning what to do about it, and counts only the outcomes that are confirmed.

ShelfTwin prototype v0.1. Demo data only: the stores, distributor, products, batches, temperatures and sales are made up. Model parameters are illustrative and to be calibrated on pilot data. No accuracy claim is made.
